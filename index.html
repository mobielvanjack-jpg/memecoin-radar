VALUE

Price × supply

Market capitalization depends on the circulating supply assumption. FDV includes more of the potential supply and is not interchangeable with circulating cap.

EXECUTION

Liquidity matters

A high valuation can coexist with a shallow trading pool. Liquidity is not guaranteed exit value, and pool figures alone cannot estimate your slippage.

ATTENTION

Popularity ≠ demand

Volume, transaction counts, paid boosts, and headlines describe activity, not fair value or future returns. Wash trading and misleading promotion are possible.
